## 摘要
我们介绍一个包含约 10,000 次雷诺平均 Navier-Stokes（RANS）模拟的数据集，这些模拟针对绕生成下压力的 NACA 4 位翼型的定常、不可压缩、二维亚声速流动，面向与汽车和赛车运动应用相关的空气动力学状态（公开获取于 https://huggingface.co/datasets/ratiolabs/downforce-airfoils）。利用该资源，我们研究用于快速流场预测的几何条件神经代理模型，比较神经场与神经 ODE、多层感知机与基于图的模型，以及若干谱几何条件化方法。我们进一步提出 Airfoil2Vec，一种翼型专用的谱几何编码器，它将联合轮廓谱与弯度和厚度的独立谱表示相结合，用于预测连续的压力场和速度场。我们通过攻角插值、对未见 NACA 4 位几何的插值与外推，以及对未见非 NACA 翼型的泛化来评估泛化能力。所得代理模型能够准确捕捉空气动力学量和定性流动特征，同时相较传统计算流体力学提供数量级的速度提升。

## Abstract
We introduce a dataset of approximately 10,000 Reynolds-Averaged Navier-Stokes (RANS) simulations of steady, incompressible, two-dimensional subsonic flow around downforce-generating NACA 4-digit airfoils, targeting aerodynamic regimes relevant to automotive and motorsport applications (openly available on https://huggingface.co/datasets/ratiolabs/downforce-airfoils). Using this resource, we study geometry-conditioned neural surrogates for fast flow prediction, comparing neural fields with neural ODEs, MLPs with graph-based models, and several spectral geometry-conditioning methods. We further propose Airfoil2Vec, an airfoil-specific spectral geometry encoder that combines the joint contour spectrum with separate spectral representations of camber and thickness, for predicting continuous pressure and velocity fields. We evaluate generalization through angle-of-attack interpolation, interpolation and extrapolation to unseen NACA 4-digit geometries, and generalization to unseen non-NACA airfoils. The resulting surrogate accurately captures aerodynamic quantities and qualitative flow features while providing orders-of-magnitude speedups over conventional computational fluid dynamics.
