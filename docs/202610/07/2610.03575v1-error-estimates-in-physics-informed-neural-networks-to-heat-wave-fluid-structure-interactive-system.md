## 摘要
我们研究一个耦合偏微分方程系统的物理信息神经网络（PINN）逼近，该系统模拟流体与弹性动力学之间的相互作用。此处考虑的标准热-波模型通过固定边界界面上的传输条件耦合一个抛物型方程和一个双曲型方程，并作为实际应用中出现的 Stokes-弹性系统的原型。这种耦合使得误差分析比单个偏微分方程更为精细。我们用相关的 PINN 残差界定了精确解与 PINN 解之间的误差，并进一步证明泛化误差由训练误差、求积点数量和网络规模控制。据我们所知，这是首个针对耦合流体-结构相互作用偏微分方程系统的 PINN 逼近的误差估计。我们还针对给定的测试问题展示了数值实验。

## Abstract
We study a physics-informed neural network (PINN) approximation of a coupled PDE system that models the interaction between fluid and elastic dynamics. The canonical heat-wave model considered here couples a parabolic and a hyperbolic equation through transmission conditions on a fixed boundary interface, and serves as a prototype for the Stokes-elastic systems that arises in real world applications. This coupling makes the error analysis more delicate than that for a single PDE. We bound the error between the exact and PINN solutions by the associated PINN residuals, and we moreover prove that the generalization error is controlled by the training error, the number of quadrature points, and the size of the network. {To the best of our knowledge}, these are the first error estimates for a PINN approximation of a coupled fluid-structure interaction PDE system. We also show that numerical experiments with respect to a given test problem.
