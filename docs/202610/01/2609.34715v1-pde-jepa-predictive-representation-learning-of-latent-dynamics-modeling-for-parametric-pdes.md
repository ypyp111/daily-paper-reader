## 摘要
物理轨迹不仅仅是系统的快照：它们还揭示了系统状态在控制条件下如何演化。然而，参数化偏微分方程（PDE）的表征学习在很大程度上仍依赖于基于重构的目标，这些目标强调恢复观测到的物理场。在本文中，我们研究了预测式表征预训练作为基于重构的学习的一种替代方案。我们发现，预测式表征保留了丰富的物理信息，但仅凭这一优势并不能保证准确的场演化。基于这些观察，我们提出了 PDE-JEPA 用于参数化 PDE 动力学。具体而言，我们首先使用掩码潜变量预测训练一个编码器，以捕获 PDE 动力学的基本规律。为了明确地将预训练表征适配到更符合动力学的状态空间，我们随后引入一个几何投影器，将潜轨迹几何与物理场的演化几何对齐。最后，基于这一几何对齐的潜空间，我们进一步开发了一个物理结构化的潜预测器，将动力学分解为参数无关的演化分量和参数相关的响应分量。在九个广泛使用的 PDE 基准上的大量实验表明，我们的框架在分布内平均优于现有最先进方法 33.4%，而在外推到未见控制参数时平均提升 51.4%。项目页面见 https://tanpig-x.github.io/PDE-JEPA/。

## Abstract
Physical trajectories contain more than snapshots of a system: they also reveal how its states evolve under governing conditions. However, representation learning for parametric partial differential equations (PDEs) has largely relied on reconstruction-based objectives that emphasize recovering observed physical fields. In this paper, we investigate predictive representation pretraining as an alternative to reconstruction-based learning. We find that predictive representations preserve rich physical information, yet this advantage alone does not ensure accurate field evolution. Based on these observations, we introduce PDE-JEPA for parametric PDE dynamics. Specifically, we first train an encoder using a masked-latent prediction to capture the underlying regularities of PDE dynamics. To explicitly adapt the pretrained representation toward a more dynamics-aligned state space, we then introduce a geometry projector that aligns latent trajectory geometry with the evolution geometry of physical fields. Finally, building on this geometry-aligned latent space, we further develop a physics-structured latent predictor that decomposes the dynamics into parameter-independent evolution and parameter-dependent response components. Extensive experiments on nine widely used PDE benchmarks demonstrate that our framework outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods by an average of 33.4\% in-distribution, while achieving an average improvement of 51.4\% when extrapolating to unseen governing parameters.
