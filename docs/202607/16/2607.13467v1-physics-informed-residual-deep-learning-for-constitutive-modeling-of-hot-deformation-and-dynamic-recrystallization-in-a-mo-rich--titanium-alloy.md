## 摘要
准确的本构建模对于先进结构合金的热机械工艺设计至关重要。传统的Arrhenius型和经验模型无法充分捕捉宽工艺条件下应变硬化、动态恢复(DRV)和动态再结晶(DRX)的联合效应。本研究开发了两种堆叠残差物理信息神经网络(STAR-PINNs)来模拟富Mo α+β钛合金(Ti-6Al-4Mo-1V-0.1Si)的热变形响应。增强型STAR-PINN结合了热机械本构约束，而DRX感知型STAR-PINN采用双输出架构来考虑再结晶动力学。两种模型均使用共享残差编码器，该编码器在温度800至1050°C、应变速率0.01至10 s⁻¹范围内采集的实验流动应力数据上训练。通过自动微分施加物理信息约束，包括热软化、应变速率敏感性、应变硬化和峰后软化。DRX感知模型进一步集成了JMAK-Avrami正则化、DRX饱和约束以及基于Arrhenius的可调参数一致性，通过潜在特征融合将预测的DRX分数直接链接到应力输出。DRX感知型STAR-PINN的RMSE为11.69 MPa，MAE为4.83 MPa，R²为0.9850，交叉验证的RMSE为12.47±0.26 MPa。该模型准确再现了温度依赖的流动曲线、DRX动力学和Zener-Hollomon关系，同时保持了物理一致的本构行为。这些结果表明，物理驱动深度学习为构建本构模型提供了稳健且可解释的框架，为钛合金的先进工艺建模提供了实用方法。

## Abstract
Accurate constitutive modeling of hot deformation behavior is essential for designing thermomechanical processes in advanced structural alloys. Conventional Arrhenius-type and empirical models do not adequately capture the combined effects of strain hardening, dynamic recovery (DRV), and dynamic recrystallization (DRX) across broad processing conditions. In this study, two Stacked Residual Physics-Informed Neural Networks (STAR-PINNs) were developed to simulate the hot deformation response of a Mo-rich $α+β$ titanium alloy (Ti-6Al-4Mo-1V-0.1Si). The Enhanced STAR-PINN incorporated thermomechanical constitutive constraints, while the DRX-Aware STAR-PINN employed a dual-output architecture to account for recrystallization kinetics. Both models used a shared residual encoder trained on experimental flow stress data collected at temperatures from 800 to 1050 degrees C and strain rates between 0.01 and 10 per second. Physics-informed constraints, including thermal softening, strain-rate sensitivity, strain hardening, and post-peak softening, were enforced through automatic differentiation. The DRX-Aware model further integrated JMAK-Avrami regularization, DRX saturation constraints, and Arrhenius-based consistency with tunable parameters, directly linking the predicted DRX fraction to stress output via latent-feature fusion. The DRX-Aware STAR-PINN achieved RMSE = 11.69 MPa, MAE = 4.83 MPa, R^2 = 0.9850, and a cross-validated RMSE of 12.47 +/- 0.26 MPa. This model accurately reproduced temperature-dependent flow curves, DRX kinetics, and Zener-Hollomon relationships, while maintaining physically consistent constitutive behavior. These results demonstrate that physics-informed deep learning provides a robust and interpretable framework for constitutive modeling, offering a practical approach for advanced process modeling of titanium alloys.
