## 摘要
我们研究了用于求解带有 Dirichlet 边界条件的二阶椭圆型偏微分方程的量子物理信息神经网络（QPINNs）的梯度流训练动力学。我们将参数化量子电路视为函数逼近器，并通过神经正切核（NTK）的视角分析其过参数化情形。我们的贡献是面向 QPINNs 及其变分形式的非渐近惰性训练理论：我们证明，当电路宽度足够大时，非线性梯度流可由线性化 NTK 模型定量近似，并给出依赖于量子比特数、电路深度、电路光锥几何以及 PDE 解的定义域维数的显式界。

## Abstract
We study the gradient-flow training dynamics of quantum physics-informed neural networks (QPINNs) for the solution of second-order elliptic partial differential equations with Dirichlet boundary conditions. We consider parameterized quantum circuits as function approximators and analyze their overparameterized regime through the lens of the neural tangent kernel (NTK). Our contribution is a nonasymptotic lazy-training theory for QPINNs and their variational formulation: we prove that, for sufficiently large circuit width, the nonlinear gradient flow is quantitatively approximated by a linearized NTK model, with explicit bounds depending on the number of qubits, circuit depth, circuit light-cone geometry, and the dimension of the domain of the solution to the PDE.

## 2. 方法论

### 2.1 模型与训练框架
- **量子电路**：$m$ 个量子比特，$L$ 层，$U(\Theta,x)=U_L\cdots U_1$；每层由参数化单比特门 $W_\ell(\Theta)$（$W_i(\theta_i)=e^{-iG_i\theta_i}$，$\theta_i\in[0,\pi]$）与输入编码/固定纠缠门 $V_\ell(x)$ 组成，参数总数 $|\Theta|=Lm$。
- **网络输出**：$u(\Theta,x)=\frac{1}{b(m)}\langle 0^m|U^\dagger(\Theta,x)\,O\,U(\Theta,x)|0^m\rangle$，$O=\sum_k O_k$ 为单比特可观测之和，$b(m)$ 为归一化因子。
- **损失与梯度流**：$L(\Theta)=\tfrac12\sum_{i}(A u(\Theta,x^{(i)})-f(x^{(i)}))^2+\tfrac12\sum_{j}(u(\Theta,\hat x^{(j)})-g(\hat x^{(j)}))^2$，梯度流 $\dot\Theta_t=-\eta\nabla_\Theta L$，学习率 $\eta=\eta_0/b_K(m)$。
- **线性化模型**：$U_{\rm lin}(\Theta_t^{\rm lin},z)=U(\Theta_0,z)+\nabla_\Theta U(\Theta_0,z)(\Theta_t^{\rm lin}-\Theta_0)$，其损失为 $L_{\rm lin}=\tfrac12\|U_{\rm lin}-Y\|_2^2$，对应线性 ODE 有闭式解 $U_{\rm lin}(\Theta_t^{\rm lin},Z)=Y+e^{-\eta_0\hat K_{\Theta_0}t}(U(\Theta_0,Z)-Y)$。
