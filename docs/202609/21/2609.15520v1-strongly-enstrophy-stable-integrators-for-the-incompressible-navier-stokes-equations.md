## 摘要
我们为不可压缩 Navier-Stokes 方程提出了一种混合有限元离散化，它在比先前离散化更强的意义下保持能量和拟涡能的演化律。在二维情形下，拟涡能的演化律对热力学孤立系统只允许耗散，这导致速度梯度的与雷诺数无关的界，从而自然地稳定该格式，即使在严重欠解析的网格上也是如此。在三维情形下，该格式同时保持耗散以及通过涡旋拉伸产生的拟涡能。我们通过系统地在离散化中引入辅助变量来强制这些演化律成立。尽管这些格式的协调实现需要具有增强正则性的离散 Stokes 复形，但我们引入了 (i) 等价重参数化和 (ii) 罚公式，它们仅需要标准离散 de Rham 复形中典型的 curl 协调和 div 协调空间。该格式可处理不同类型的边界条件和弯曲区域。所提格式的稳健稳定性质通过剪切流、球形涡和绕障碍物流动的数值模拟得到展示。我们在数值上观察到，以这种方式保持拟涡能的离散演化对数值解具有很强的稳定作用，尤其是在二维情形下。

## Abstract
We propose a mixed finite element discretisation for the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations that preserves the evolution laws of both energy and enstrophy, in a stronger sense than previous discretisations. In two dimensions, the evolution law for enstrophy only permits dissipation for thermodynamically isolated systems, leading to a Reynolds-number-independent bound on the velocity gradient that naturally stabilises the scheme, even on severely under-resolved meshes. In three dimensions, the scheme preserves both dissipation and the generation of enstrophy through vortex stretching. We enforce these evolution laws by systematically introducing auxiliary variables into the discretisation. While conforming implementations of these schemes require discrete Stokes complexes with enhanced regularity, we introduce both (i) equivalent reparametrisations and (ii) penalty formulations that require only the typical curl- and div-conforming spaces from the standard discrete de Rham complex. The scheme handles different types of boundary conditions and curved domains. The robust stabilisation properties of the proposed scheme are demonstrated through numerical simulations of a shear flow, a spherical vortex, and flow past an obstacle. We observe numerically that preserving the discrete evolution of enstrophy in this way has a strong stabilising effect on the numerical solution, especially in two dimensions.
