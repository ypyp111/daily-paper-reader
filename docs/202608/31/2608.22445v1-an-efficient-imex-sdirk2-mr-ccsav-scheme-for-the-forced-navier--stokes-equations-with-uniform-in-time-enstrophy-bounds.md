## 摘要
我们提出并分析了一种用于涡量形式的受迫二维周期纳维-斯托克斯方程的IMEX-SDIRK2均值回复并发校正标量辅助变量（mr-ccSAV）方法。粘性项采用Alexander的SDIRK2方法处理，平流项显式处理。每个阶段需要求解两个具有相同移位拉普拉斯算子的椭圆方程，以及一个三次或线性标量代数方程。对于初始涡量在$\dot L^s(Ω)$，$s>2$中，每个正时间步长都存在阶段解；唯一性在显式小步长条件下单独建立。主要结果是对任意正时间步长的直接、无条件时间一致涡量界。对于持续有界的强迫项，该估计是吸收的：初始数据的影响衰减，强迫项的贡献不随时间累积。在额外正则性、一致有界步长和有界相邻步长比下，我们还在没有小步长条件的情况下建立了时间一致的$H^1$和$H^2$涡量界。对于光滑解，该方法以二阶最优收敛。数值实验证实了其准确性、长期鲁棒性以及伴随嵌入式时间步长选择器的有效性。

## Abstract
We propose and analyze an IMEX-SDIRK2 mean-reverting concurrent-correction scalar auxiliary variable (mr-ccSAV) method for the forced two-dimensional periodic Navier--Stokes equations in vorticity form. The viscous term is treated by Alexander's SDIRK2 method and advection explicitly. Each stage requires two elliptic solves with the same shifted Laplacian and the solution of either a cubic or a linear scalar algebraic equation. For initial vorticity in $\dot L^s(Ω)$, $s>2$, a stage solution exists for every positive time step; uniqueness is established separately under an explicit small-step condition. The principal result is a direct, unconditional uniform-in-time enstrophy bound for arbitrary positive time steps. For persistently bounded forcing, this estimate is absorbing: the influence of the initial data decays, and the forcing contribution does not accumulate in time. Under additional regularity, uniformly bounded step sizes, and bounded neighboring step ratios, we also establish uniform-in-time $H^1$ and $H^2$ vorticity bounds without a small-step condition. For smooth solutions, the method converges optimally at second order. Numerical experiments confirm its accuracy, long-time robustness, and effectiveness of a companion embedded time-step selector.
